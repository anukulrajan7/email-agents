# Status, 15 Oct 2026

Honest status of Post Room. Replaces `status-2026-10-14.md`. For what each feature does, see `docs/features.md`; for how
it fits together, `docs/architecture.md`.

In one line: the product is a working, safe, cost-controlled mail assistant with packs, a team of helpers that earn
freedom, HRMS desks, and AI health monitoring. It is not yet something to hand to a stranger without a person watching the
first weeks: model quality on real mail is proven offline, not yet on a live baseline.

## After v1

1. **Smarter matching.** An LLM rerank on top of the explained score, and attachment extraction beyond five files.
2. **Members connect their own Gmail.** Today an admin connects each mailbox; a per-person consent flow is missing.
3. **Live golden baseline.** The scenario set runs offline in CI. A recorded live run against real models, with scores kept as the bar, is not done.
4. **Understanding v2 switch.** v2 (one call instead of two, about 75% cheaper labelling) runs in shadow. Switch `UNDERSTAND_V2=on` when the AI health page shows at least 95% agreement and "ready to switch".
5. **Ask Meera on HR numbers.** The metric functions exist; the assistant does not call them yet.
6. **Restore drill.** Run backups restore and migrations on a copy of production in CI.
7. **Scheduled performance summary** and a "correction needed" alert kind.

## Known limits

- Backfill is bounded (14 days, 100 mails by default) and older mail is understood when opened.
- Cost-per-email "after" figures are estimates; the AI health page shows the measured number.
- Everything above was verified with offline stubs for AI and simulated Gmail; real-model quality is not yet measured.
